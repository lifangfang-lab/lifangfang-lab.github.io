About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Biomedical Engineering at the School of Medicine, Nanjing University. My research sits at the intersection of medicine and engineering, where I use computational methods to address problems in medical imaging and clinical diagnosis.

I am passionate about translating engineering tools into practical solutions that help clinicians make better decisions, and I enjoy collaborating with researchers from both medical and technical backgrounds.

Research

My research develops computational and deep-learning frameworks to improve medical image analysis and disease diagnosis. Current directions include:

🩻

Medical Image Analysis

Developing segmentation, registration and classification methods for clinical imaging data such as CT, MRI and ultrasound.

🤖

Deep Learning for Diagnosis

Building deep learning models that assist clinicians with earlier and more accurate diagnosis of disease.

🧬

Biomedical Signal Processing

Analyzing physiological signals (ECG, EEG, etc.) to extract biomarkers for monitoring and prediction.

⚙️

Computational Modeling

Combining physics-based models with data-driven methods to simulate complex biological systems.
